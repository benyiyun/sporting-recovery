Our Programmes

All our programmes are completely free, delivered by qualified coaches with mental health training. A referral from a GP or health professional is required to join.

How to Join

A simple, supported pathway to get you started.

🏥

Step one

Talk to Your GP or Health Professional

Ask your GP, mental health nurse, social worker, or care coordinator about a referral to Sporting Recovery.

📋

Step two

They Submit the Referral Form

Your health professional completes our short online referral form. It takes around five minutes — no lengthy paperwork.

🔍

Step three

We Review Your Referral

Our team reviews the referral and gets in touch with you for a friendly, no-pressure conversation about your needs.

✉️

Step four

Email Confirmation of Acceptance

Once accepted, you’ll receive an email confirmation with everything you need to know before your first session.

🏃

Step five

Attend Any of Our Three Venues

You’re welcome at Lambeth, Southwark, and Lewisham — come to whichever session suits you each week.

Our Wellness Café

Our Wellness Café prioritises mental health and provides a supportive and inclusive environment through group discussions, mindfulness exercises, and peer support networks.

The Wellness Café exists because participants told us they needed more time after sessions to decompress, talk and feel part of a community. Participants also introduced "Chess at the Café" as a calm, inclusive way to connect, and our Book Club started with titles recommended by clients. It is staffed by our Wellness Coach.

💬

FOR HEALTH PROFESSIONALS

Information for Health Professionals

If you work with adults experiencing mental health challenges and feel our programme could benefit your client, we'd welcome a referral. Our form is quick to complete and we handle everything from there.

✓ GPs and practice staff

✓ Mental health nurses and psychiatrists

✓ Social workers and care coordinators

✓ Community mental health teams (CMHTs)

✓ Occupational therapists and physiotherapists

✓ NHS talking therapies and IAPT staff

Submit a Referral →

Eligibility

Adults 18+ experiencing mental health challenges

Assessment

No formal assessment needed — a short referral form is all we require

Response time

We aim to contact your client within 5 working days of receiving the referral

Cost

All sessions are completely free of charge.
