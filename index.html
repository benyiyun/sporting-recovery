Recovery Through Sport and Community

Sporting Recovery CIC delivers free sports and wellness programmes across Lambeth, Southwark and Lewisham, helping people improve their mental health through the power of movement and connection.

Who We Are

Sporting Recovery is a non-profit CIC that helps people take charge of their mental health. Step onto the court or join a session. Our welcoming and energising setting helps everyone connect, get active, and break free from isolation.
