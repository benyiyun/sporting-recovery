Recovery Through Sport and Community

Sporting Recovery CIC delivers free sports and wellness programmes across Lambeth, Southwark and Lewisham, helping people improve their mental health through the power of movement and connection.

Who We Are

Sporting Recovery is a non-profit CIC that helps people take charge of their mental health. Step onto the court or join a session. Our welcoming and energising setting helps everyone connect, get active, and break free from isolation.

WHO WE SUPPORT

Our community is vibrant and inclusive, welcoming people from all backgrounds and identities. Although two-thirds of our members are men between 22 and 65 facing mental health challenges, our programmes are open to all adults seeking support. Sporting Recovery is for everyone, and together we break isolation and rebuild confidence.

Every week, our coaches and volunteers welcome over 100 adults into stigma-free Wellness Programmes. The results speak for themselves: two-thirds stay six months or longer, and nearly half continue for over a year.

WHERE WE'RE FROM

Sporting Recovery started as a clinical trial in Southwark, supporting men with severe mental illness. In 2014, driven by local passion, it became a Community Interest Company open to all adults facing mental health challenges.

WHAT WE DO

Our programme energises the community with Badminton, Yoga, Dance, Tennis, Basketball, Football, Gym, and our Wellness Café. Every session is free, so cost is never a barrier. Join us: Tuesdays in Lambeth, Wednesdays in Southwark, or Thursdays in Lewisham, from 1:00 to 4:00 PM.
